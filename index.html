GLOBAL AI GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Certified AI Governance

The primary international standard for integrating and managing artificial intelligence in industry is ISO/IEC 42001:2023. It provides a certifiable framework for establishing an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS), helping organizations responsibly develop, deploy, and govern AI technologies across manufacturing, logistics, and institutional sectors.

ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS FRAMEWORK

AI Management System (AIMS)

Specifies requirements for building, maintaining, and continually improving an organizational AI governance and management framework tailored for enterprise operations.

ISO/IEC 23894 RISK MANAGEMENT

AI Risk Management Guidance

Offers practical guidance on managing specific risks, failure modes, and safety hazards tied to artificial intelligence projects and operational computer vision strategies.

ISO/IEC 5338 LIFECYCLE

AI System Lifecycle Processes

Defines robust engineering lifecycle processes specifically designed for neural network deployment, continuous verification, validation, and maintenance.

ISO/IEC 23053 FRAMEWORK & VOCAB

Machine Learning Framework

Establishes a common terminology, architectural framework, and computational vocabulary for machine learning, pipeline auditing, and AI system components.

Key Strategic Benefits of ISO AI Integration

Risk Mitigation: Systematically addresses unique threats like data bias, security flaws, false positives, and model drift in physical plants.
Regulatory Compliance: Aligns internal industrial processes with emerging global rules, the EU AI Act, and national data protection mandates.
Traceability & Auditing: Improves documentation, incident playback, and telemetry across the entire machine learning and operational pipeline.
Stakeholder Trust: Provides verifiable, third-party auditable proof of responsible, transparent, and ethical AI usage across all facilities.
